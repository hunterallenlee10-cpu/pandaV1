# Review carousel

This folder holds the review carousel that every customer-review carousel on the site now uses:

| Where | What it replaced | Version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The "Testimonials" section on `/about/`, `/commercial-capabilities/`, `/commercial-roofing/` (and its two sub-pages), `/gutters/gutter-guards/`, `/podcast/`, `/roofing/` (and its five sub-pages), `/roofing-costs/`, `/solar/` (and its two sub-pages) | a Google-reviews carousel of two reviews that never started (its script ran before the carousel library loaded), so only the first review showed and the arrows did nothing; an earlier fix showed the two side by side | up to three reviews in view |
| Beside the video in "Panda Exteriors Is Your Top Roofing Choice" on `/` and `/services/` | a one-review carousel whose reviews were all pushed to the height of the longest one, leaving a large empty band between the arrows and every shorter review | one review in view, on green |
| The same place on `/thank-you/` | the two-review Google carousel again, shown as two tall stacked cards | one review in view, on green |

The pages are edited by `scripts/lib/reviews.mjs` (called from `scripts/lib/site-fixes.mjs`) during
`scripts/03-build.mjs`, so re-running the capture and build reproduces them. `SITE_FIXES=0` builds the original sections.

## What it does

- **A real carousel.** It loops endlessly in both directions, and moves on by itself every 6.5 seconds; the active
  dot fills up to show when. Arrows, dots, the arrow keys, dragging with the mouse or a finger, and sideways trackpad
  swipes all move it. Every movement glides on a spring, so quick clicks, drags and flicks blend into one smooth
  motion instead of jumping.
- **Holds still when it should:** while the mouse is over it, while it has keyboard focus, while a review is expanded,
  while it is off screen and while the tab is hidden. The pause button (left of the dots) stops it for good.
- **Every card the same size.** Long reviews show the first few lines with a "Read more" button; shorter cards keep the
  same height, so the row lines up and nothing below it jumps as the reviews change.
- Reviewers without a photo get their initials. The stars, quote mark and colours follow the site's green and orange.
- **Accessible:** it is a labelled carousel region with real buttons; reviews out of view cannot be reached with the
  Tab key or read out by screen readers, and changes are announced when it is not playing by itself. People who prefer
  reduced motion get no autoplay and no gliding.
- **Works without JavaScript:** all reviews are in the page; they then show in full, in a row that scrolls sideways.

## Files

| File | |
| --- | --- |
| `reviews.json` | **The content: the reviews, in the order shown.** Edit this. |
| `reviews.css`, `reviews.js` | Styles and the script (no dependencies). Copied to `site/_custom/reviews/` by the build. |

The markup is generated by `scripts/lib/reviews.mjs`.

## Editing the reviews

Every carousel shows all the reviews in `reviews.json`, in its order. Each review has a `name` and its `text`;
`rating` is the number of stars (1–5, 5 if left out) and `photo` is an optional picture on the site (a path such as
`/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Tonya-perry.png`). Without a photo, the reviewer's initials are shown. The list holds the
reviews that were on the site: the ten from the home page carousel and the two from the Google reviews section (one of
them, Tracey Duval's, is in both).

After editing, rebuild: `node scripts/03-build.mjs` (or the whole pipeline with `bash scripts/capture-all.sh`). Without
the capture cache (a fresh clone), run `node scripts/tools/update-built-site.mjs` instead: it renders the carousels in
`site/` again from `reviews.json` and copies the stylesheet and script.
