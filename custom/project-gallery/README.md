# Project gallery

This folder holds the "Our Project Gallery" section on `/`, `/solar/`, `/roofing/`, `/roofing/types/`, `/gutters/`,
`/siding/`, `/commercial-capabilities/` and `/commercial-roofing/`.

As delivered it was a Breakdance gallery (category tabs over a slider) that three scripts started at once: Breakdance's
own slider, the theme's `new Swiper('.Project-swipper')` on its wrapper and the theme's `new Swiper('.swiper')` (a 2.5 s
autoplay meant for the logo row) on its inner box. They fought over the same photos, so the photos came out at
different widths with the first one cut off, the row sat off centre under the heading and tabs, and there was a dot for
every photo of every category (the rendered snapshots also carried six extra copies of photos). `/gallery/` shows the
same photos as a full grid, which works, and is left as it is.

The pages are edited by `scripts/lib/project-gallery.mjs` (called from `scripts/lib/site-fixes.mjs`) during
`scripts/03-build.mjs`, so re-running the capture and build reproduces them. `SITE_FIXES=0` builds the original section.

## What it does

- **The same tabs and photos, tidied up.** The tabs (Roofing, Solar, Commercial, Gutters, Community and Charity) are
  centred and show how many photos each has. Every photo is the same size (4:3, cropped to fill), with even gaps: three
  in view on computers, two on tablets, one on phones, lined up exactly under the heading.
- **Easy to move through.** The arrows move a whole view at a time and the dots jump to a view (with more than ten
  views, a "4 / 22" count shows instead). The row also scrolls by swiping, with the trackpad, or by dragging with the
  mouse, and always settles on whole photos. It does not move by itself.
- **A photo viewer.** A photo opens full size over a dark screen: arrows, the arrow keys and swiping step through the
  category (looping), and Esc, the close button or a click beside the photo closes it. The page behind stays put, and
  the row then shows the last photo seen.
- **Accessible:** real tabs (arrow keys, Home and End move between them), labelled buttons and photo links, and the
  focus returns to the photo when the viewer closes. People who prefer reduced motion get no gliding.
- **Works without JavaScript:** every photo is in one row that scrolls sideways, and each opens the full-size picture.

## Files

| File | |
| --- | --- |
| `project-gallery.css`, `project-gallery.js` | Styles and the script (no dependencies). Copied to `site/_custom/project-gallery/` by the build. |

The markup is generated by `scripts/lib/project-gallery.mjs` from the gallery in the page (its tabs and photos, in
order). A page built earlier is rendered again from its own gallery, so after editing the markup there, or the
stylesheet or script here, `node scripts/tools/update-built-site.mjs` updates `site/` without the capture cache.
